# Repose planner v9.2

V9.2 repairs the autonomous-approach deadlock seen in both sim2sim and
`bags/04Sep2026_04_46`. It retains v9.1 perception, belief, scanning, clip
selection and reference interpolation unchanged.

## Evidence and behavior

The failed rollout correctly read red, performed `R#57`, then correctly read
green. Its 0.17 m approach produced -0.04 m measured progress. Because v9.1
allowed one no-progress result, the planner emitted `approach blocked` forever.

V9.2 makes the following bounded changes:

- `enter_forward_m: 0.25`: the observed +0.24 m entry residual goes directly
  to the manipulation policy.
- `min_window_m: 0.30`: the source's incomplete 0.17 m prefix is removed; its
  first two eligible bouts are approximately 0.35 m and 0.41 m.
- `no_progress_limit: 2`: one failed bout may retry, matching
  `max_attempts: 2`.
- `escalate_window: true`: the retry uses the next longer gait, clamped to the
  vocabulary's longest gait.
- `forward_only: true`: a required entry stance behind the robot becomes
  `reposition required`; the forward gait is never asked to make it worse.
- `latch_blocked: true`: terminal safety holds stay engaged without repeatedly
  recommitting identical stand references. A ready pose, new rung/target,
  re-arm, or operator repositioning releases the hold through normal state
  reset/re-evaluation.

Manual joystick walking in disarmed STAND remains a separate 1 m bout.

Acceptance cases:

1. Entry-forward residual at or below 0.25 m: direct manipulation, no walk.
2. Macro residual: no 0.17 m plan; first approach is about 0.35 m.
3. Defeat the first approach by dragging the cube: exactly one approximately
   0.41 m retry.
4. Defeat both: one stable `approach blocked` hold with no reference churn.
