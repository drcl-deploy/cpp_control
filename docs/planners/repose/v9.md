# Repose v9

V9 keeps the v8 depth-first square localization and v8.5 approach motion. It
changes the planner seams that were visible in the hardware rollout
`bags/04Sep2026_02_04`: candidate discontinuities, yaw steps, coarse approach
admission, cancelling scans, and repeated walks without measured progress.

## Behavior changes

- A selected clip row is held until the act commits. Its absolute entry heading
  is held across the square pose's modulo-90-degree fold; only the integer cube
  symmetry changes at the fold.
- SCAN uses the same 45 deg/s, 180 deg/s² acceleration-limited shape as stand
  yaw. Blind search is a relative `+90, -180, -90` sequence, so the return pass
  visits new headings instead of cancelling the first turn. A close partial
  top gets a 12-degree reframe instead of a full search.
- CLIP and APPROACH both publish a short joint-and-heading ENTER act, re-engage
  on the pose actually reached, and then publish the unmodified source motion.
- Approach readiness is axis-wise: the desired entry displacement must be
  within 0.18 m forward and 0.10 m lateral. A walk targets 0.08 m forward
  residual. Walk windows are cut by net displacement, matching the quantity
  used by admission and feedback.
- Post-walk progress is measured against the same row and physical symmetry
  selected before the walk. Less than 0.05 m progress blocks another blind
  repeat for that rung.
- PLANE still fits geometry from valid depth, but color votes are pooled by
  projecting RGB rays into the fitted square. Missing depth no longer deletes
  otherwise valid top-face color pixels.

The v8 and v8.5 presets retain their old gates, scan profile, candidate
selection, and cumulative-path walk windows for direct A/B runs.

## Disarmed stand controls

- Right stick X: unlimited heading yaw, as before.
- Left stick forward: one finite walk bout. The controller plays lead-in, the
  bounded source slice, lead-out, then a nominal-stand pause. If the stick is
  still held it starts another complete bout; it never rewinds mid-stride.
- Yaw and walking are mutually exclusive. A walk waits for stand yaw to stop.
- `A` is refused until the left stick is released and the walk has returned to
  nominal stand. `RB` immediately disarms and restores nominal stand.
- Reverse and lateral walking are intentionally unsupported.

The manual walk is an operator setup aid only. It is disabled once the planner
is armed and does not affect autonomous approach accounting.

## Sim2sim

```bash
source ~/unitree_ros2/setup_local.sh
export VIBE_ASSET_ROOT=/home/loki/drcl_projects/vibe_wip
cfg="$(ros2 pkg prefix cpp_control)/share/cpp_control/config/repose_planner/g1_sim_v9.yaml"

ros2 launch cpp_control g1_repose_planner.launch.py \
  artifact_dir:=/home/loki/drcl_projects/vibe_wip/logs/rsl_rl/g1_repose_big_cube_floor/wandb_checkpoints/011pgzbh \
  env:=sim planner_config:="$cfg"
```

Start in RB stand. Test right-stick yaw, then release it. Hold left-stick
forward through at least two complete bouts and verify each bout visibly
returns to nominal before repeating. Release the stick and verify `A` arms only
after the lead-out has finished. Then drag the cube forward, laterally, behind
the robot, and close to the camera during a rollout.
